## Brief

**Feature:** "Anywhere", date-led destination discovery for Booking.com.

**Goal of the design:** help travellers who know *when* but not *where* feel inspired **and** confident enough to book, without leaving the familiar Booking.com experience.

**Mood / keywords:** familiar (nothing to relearn) · trustworthy (real availability, total prices) · light (lots of white, photos do the work) · factual (summaries built from real bookings and reviews)

**Approach: evolve, don't redesign**
- The MVP keeps Booking.com's existing colours, type, shapes and components. A redesign would change two things at once.
- New UI only where the feature is new: the "Anywhere" suggestion, the destination card (Screen 2), the destination overview (Screen 3) and the "via Anywhere" tag.
- Everything else is reused as it is: search box, filter panel, property card, map, buttons.

**Visual direction**
- Imagery: bright photos of real places and of the stays themselves, no text on images. Photos come from the most-booked properties, not from a new content library.
- Colour & type: existing Booking.com design tokens (colour values on board 1 are approximations from the current UI).
- Layout: cards that make price, availability and group fit scannable at a glance.
- Destination information: a short factual summary built from existing data (what guests mention, most booked areas, guest rating) and rule-based tags. No written descriptions.

**What to avoid**
- Pure "inspiration" visuals with no price or availability.
- Anything that looks like a new product rather than part of Booking.com.
- Written destination descriptions that need a content team (cost) or invent claims.
- New filters on Screen 3: it reuses the standard filter panel unchanged.

**Future vision (board 3):** start from a mood, a total budget with flexible dates, a map-first view, shared wish lists and personalised matching. All deliberately cut from the MVP and to be validated after the experiment.
