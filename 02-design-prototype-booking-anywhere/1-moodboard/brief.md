## Brief

**Feature:** "Anywhere", date-led destination discovery for Booking.com.

**Goal of the design:** help travellers who know *when* but not *where* feel inspired **and** confident enough to book, without leaving the familiar Booking.com experience.

**Who it's for:** Yuliya (budget and dates explorer), Tomáš (flexible couple), Lucie (flexible family). See the [personas](../../01-feature-proposal-booking-anywhere/proposal.md#5-user-personas).

**Mood / keywords:** [e.g. bright, real places, trustworthy, calm, easy to compare]

**Visual direction**
- Imagery: bright photos of real places, no text on images, 3:2 hero ratio
- Colour & type: existing Booking.com design tokens, no redesign
- Layout: cards that make price and availability scannable at a glance

**Inspiration sources:** [e.g. Google Travel Explore, Airbnb categories, Pinterest boards: what you took from each]

**What to avoid:** [e.g. pure "inspiration" visuals with no price; anything that looks like a new product rather than part of Booking.com]
